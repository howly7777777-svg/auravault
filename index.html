Privacy Policy

Your privacy is the absolute core foundation of AuraVault. Our core architecture is built upon zero-server, 100% on-device data persistence.

  • Zero Server Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, journal entries, health metrics, financial transactions, or private memories to any remote servers.
  • Local Encrypted Storage: All your data is stored locally on your Apple device using SwiftData and encrypted using AES-GCM (CryptoKit).
  • On-Device AI Processing: Natural language processing, vector embeddings, OCR document scanning, and sentiment analyses run locally on your device's Apple Silicon / Neural Engine.
  • Optional LAN VajraHowly Hub: If enabled, your iPhone communicates over your local home Wi-Fi directly with your own Mac. No data ever leaves your local network.
